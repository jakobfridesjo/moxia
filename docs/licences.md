Listening together reaches other listeners through n0's public [iroh](https://iroh.computer) relays and DNS (`iroh.link`) when it cannot connect directly; they pass the encrypted connections on and publish each room's addresses under its throwaway key, and see nothing of what is said. They are rate-limited and run by number 0 as a free service.

The streams and logos the directory points to belong to their stations; the app plays and shows them without storing anything but logos in its cache.

## Paintings

The ink layers in `src/pages/radio/ui/ink/` are cut from paintings in the [Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access](https://www.clevelandart.org/open-access) collection, released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

| Accession                                             | Painting                                                                                | Layers                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| [1985.71](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1985.71.2) | Min Zhen, album of twelve leaves, 1788                                                  | dozing-under-pine, river-landscape, su-dongpo |
| [1975.95](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1975.95)   | Luo Ping, Scholar Watching the Waterfall, hanging scroll, 18th century                  | scholar-at-waterfall                          |
| [1979.28](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/1979.28)   | Chen Chun, Looking for a Monastery in the Misty Mountains, hanging scroll, 16th century | monastery-path                                |

`mise art:ink` makes the layers from `cutouts.json` next to them: it downloads each painting's full-resolution TIFF into `.cache/ink-cutouts/` (about 1 GB the first time), refusing any that is not CC0, crops it, turns the ink into transparency in the painter's ink colour, and drops seals, the boxes in `erase` (inscriptions and signatures) and paper grain. `inkOnly` washes out the light colour of a painting in ink and colour, keeping the ink outlines under it and some of its shading; seals then go only by `erase`. `strength` below 1 keeps the ink of a painter who painted in grey from turning black. `mise art:ink <name>` redoes one layer; `.cache/ink-cutouts/preview/` shows each on paper. Take new paintings from the same collection and check their licence is CC0; prefer ink on paper, since silk has darkened too much to separate from the ink.

## Models

Downloaded at runtime from Hugging Face when subtitles are turned on or a chat message is first translated, never shipped in the app. The About page credits them.

| Model                                                                                    | Licence                                                                              | Used for                                            |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| [SenseVoice-Small](https://github.com/FunAudioLLM/SenseVoice), as k2-fsa exports it      | [FunASR Model License](https://github.com/modelscope/FunASR/blob/main/MODEL_LICENSE) | Speech recognition                                  |
| [FireRedVAD](https://huggingface.co/FireRedTeam/FireRedVAD)                              | Apache-2.0                                                                           | Finding speech, telling talk from singing and music |
| [opus-mt-zh-en](https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-zh-en), as Xenova exports it | CC BY 4.0                                                                            | Translating chat messages into English              |

The FunASR Model License allows use, copying and sharing as long as the source, authors and model names are kept; take care to keep the attribution if this list changes.

## Dependencies

Every Rust and npm dependency is under a permissive licence or MPL-2.0 (symphonia).

- The About page lists their notices, generated by `mise licenses:generate` into the gitignored `src/pages/about/model/third-party-licenses.gen.json`. mise reruns it when a lockfile changes.
- It fails on a crate whose licence is not in `accepted` in `src-tauri/about.toml`. Check the licence before adding it.
